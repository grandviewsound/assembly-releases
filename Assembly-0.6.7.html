Assembly 0.6.7

Aligning lavs to the boom:

  • Align Selected Clips no longer refuses clips that start one sample before a frame. Assembled clips often start there, and they were being left alone with the reason "no exact start in its roll".
  • A report track for alignments. A track named like ALN Report marks each place worth looking at:
    • NOT ALIGNED: refused before any audio was read.
    • LEFT ALONE: read, and deliberately not changed.
    • FAILED: could not be rendered.
    • CHECK: aligned, but listen here first.
    • Each take gets one entry saying how many clips and why, and Save Diagnostic Report… lists every clip behind it.
    • Turn it off with Report in the Align sheet.
  • When nothing can be aligned, an alert says why, where before there was only a line in the status bar.
