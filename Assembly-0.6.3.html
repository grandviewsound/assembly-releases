Assembly 0.6.3

Picture tracks from an EDL:

  • Scene tracks from scene-first slates. On slates like 15F-2A, the letters after the scene number are now read as the setup, so a scene cut across its setups is one scene clip instead of dozens. Slates with a unit's letter in front (V15J) join the scene they are cut into; inserted scenes (A74) keep their own number.
  • Shot colors by angle follow the setup. Every take and camera of one setup now shares a color.
  • Leaders stay off the Scene track. A head or tail leader keyed over the neighboring reel's shots no longer shows that reel's scene. The Shot track is unchanged.
  • Television reels. A list whose leader runs up to first frame of action on the hour (00:59:52:00) is no longer offered a placement an hour out.
