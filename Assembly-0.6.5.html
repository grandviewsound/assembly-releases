Assembly 0.6.5

Aligning lavs to the boom:

  • Batch Align Sound Rolls (Experimental). This aligns the lavs of whole sound rolls, with no Pro Tools session. Tick the folders to align, choose a destination, and each roll is written there in the same folder structure, with your file-name text added: C12P02T01.WAV becomes C12P02T01-ALN.WAV.
    • Every channel and all of the roll's metadata are kept. Each copy gets a new UMID, so Pro Tools never relinks it as the original roll.
    • The sound rolls themselves are only ever read.
    • Rolls with nothing to align can be copied over unchanged, so the destination holds every roll.
    • Stop keeps every finished roll, and Align carries on where it left off.
    • A roll too long for this Mac's memory is listed with the reason instead of being aligned. That's over 12 minutes on 8 GB, or 24 minutes on 16 GB.
    • After a run, Save Diagnostic Report… shows what happened to each channel, with each new roll checked against its original.
    • Start with a small folder, a day or a card, and listen to it before doing the rest.
  • Three alignment modes, in Align Selected Clips and the batch alike:
    • Boom 1: everything aligns to the take's Boom 1, as before.
    • Most prominent boom (Experimental): moment to moment, each lav follows whichever boom is carrying the scene, crossfading where that changes. The booms are left as recorded.
    • Most prominent boom, booms aligned (Experimental): the same, and the booms are also aligned to each other.
    A boom that hears nothing the take's other mics hear never leads, however loud it is. A take with one boom aligns the same way in every mode.
  • An Align button in the toolbar offers Align Selected Clips and Batch Align Sound Rolls.
  • Align Selected Clips is now marked experimental.
