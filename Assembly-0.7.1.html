Assembly 0.7.1

New: Resync Selected Clips. Sound rolls you bring in yourself with Field Recorder guide tracks can sit a little off the reference. Select the reference clip and the roll clips under it, then choose Session ▸ Resync Selected Clips…, or Resync in the Match button. Assembly measures each take against the reference by its audio and puts it back in sync, to the sample.

  • Clips stay where they are. Each roll clip is replaced at the same position and length, playing from the right place in its roll, so its edges stay on the picture cuts.
  • A summary comes first. It says how far out each take is and what will change before anything is written. Edit ▸ Undo in Pro Tools takes it back.
  • Takes with fades are left alone for now, and the summary says so.
  • New clips are named and colored the way Assemble names and colors them. Clip gain on the old clips isn't kept.

Assembling:

  • Placement is exact in more cases. A sync measurement could read one sample early, which could put a short clip a sample out. It no longer does.
  • A selection that starts just after a crossfade now places its first clip at the true edit, not the middle of the fade.
  • A clip that follows a crossfade keeps its exact end, and its fade out when you recreate fades.

Also: Help now lists what's new in each version, under Settings, Reports and Updates.

Updating from before 0.7? Assembly now uses projects: the first time it opens, it asks to move your deep scan into one. Help ▸ Settings, Reports and Updates ▸ What's new lists what every version brought.
