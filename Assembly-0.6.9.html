Assembly 0.6.9

The toolbar:

  • Every button now has its name under it: Match, Align, Scene/Shot, Diagnostic and Help. Match and Scene/Shot are new, and drop down with their commands.
  • Connecting to Pro Tools has moved. Connect and Disconnect are in the Match button now, and step 2 always says whether Assembly is connected, even before a scan.

Assembling:

  • Tracks can go in a folder. A new option in the Tracks card puts an assembly's tracks in a folder track named for your prefix, such as ASM Assembly. It is off until you turn it on.
  • Stop finishes the step it is on, then stops. It used to report a connection failure at that step.
  • The custom track color no longer comes up empty. If Pro Tools does not give Assembly its palette, the picker now says so and tries again each time it opens.

Aligning:

  • The progress window says Aligning rather than Assembling.
