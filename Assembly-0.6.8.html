Assembly 0.6.8

Picture tracks:

  • Scene numbers with a letter inside them now read. Slates like 2A7-4 and V2X38H-1 were missed, which left holes on the Scene track. Scripts that number scenes this way read too.
  • A 2 pop is no longer read as a scene. 2 POP made a one-frame scene 2 at the tail.
  • A warning when a script hardly fits the cut. Assembly remembers the script for later reels, so the last show's can still be loaded. The review now warns if it names almost none of the cut's scenes. Clear removes it.

Aligning lavs to the boom:

  • The report track no longer marks flipped polarity. Turning a lav over to match its boom is routine, and one marked its whole take. The diagnostic report counts them instead.
