Assembly 0.6.6

Picture tracks:

  • Color scenes by INT and EXT. With the shooting script added, the new Scene color choice colors each scene by where its heading sets it: one color for INT, one for EXT, one for INT/EXT.
    • Click a swatch to choose its color, or click the round arrow for three new random colors, as many times as you like.
    • Shots set to Same as its scene take the scene's color, and Shades of the scene color gives each angle a shade of it.
    • A scene the script doesn't have is left uncolored.
    • Each scene its own colors scenes one by one, as before.
  • Batch export no longer waits for Pro Tools. When Assembly isn't connected, it goes straight to reading the EDLs and shows its progress. The frame rate and sample rate then start at 24 fps and 48 kHz, so check them in the review.

Also: a Help button at the right end of the main window's toolbar opens Assembly Help.
